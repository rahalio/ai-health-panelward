# Panelward — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Patient access agent and triage nurse

- As an access agent, I want the system to tell me whether this request is inside a ratified absorption policy before I offer the patient anything, so that I am never improvising a clinical decision I am not licensed to make.
- As an access agent, I want a one-click route to a licensed clinician when a patient says something that worries me, so that following my instinct is faster than following the script.
- As a triage nurse, I want the automated channel's reasoning and the patient's own words in front of me when a request escalates to me, so that I start from evidence rather than re-interviewing a frustrated patient.
- As a triage nurse, I want requests containing behavioural health or reproductive health content to arrive in my queue by default, so that sensitive disclosures are never handled by an automated path we did not design for them.

### Panel-owning physician or advanced practice provider

- As a panel physician, I want a daily digest of what was absorbed for my patients and what changed in their plan, so that continuity survives the intervention.
- As a panel physician, I want to exclude named patients or clinical situations from absorption for my panel, so that my judgement about a fragile patient overrides a population-level rule.
- As a panel physician, I want to see whether the minutes returned to me actually landed in my schedule as appointment capacity, so that I can tell whether this programme helped patients or just moved my workload.
- As a panel physician, I want to flag a disposition as unsafe in one step and see where that flag went, so that raising a concern is not a favour I do the organisation.

### Ambulatory operations director

- As an operations director, I want unmet demand quantified per site and specialty against our own access windows, so that I can argue for capacity with evidence instead of anecdote.
- As an operations director, I want to model the access effect of a proposed absorption policy before it is ratified, so that governance debates a projected outcome rather than a vendor claim.
- As an operations director, I want recovered minutes reconciled to payroll and session data, so that the finance business partner cannot dismiss the number.

### Clinical governance, safety, and equity officer

- As a governance chair, I want to ratify each scope-of-automation policy with explicit exclusion criteria, an accountable clinician, and an expiry date, so that automation never becomes a standing permission nobody reviews.
- As a patient-safety officer, I want the downstream miss rate for absorbed requests published on a fixed cadence with a look-back window per specialty, so that the safety case is measured rather than asserted.
- As a health equity officer, I want subgroup absorption and escalation rates monitored continuously with automatic suspension on breach, so that a disparity stops the policy instead of appearing in a quarterly report.
- As a governance chair, I want to suspend a policy immediately and have every in-flight absorbed request rerouted to human handling, so that a suspected safety signal costs minutes rather than a committee cycle.

### Workforce planning and finance analyst

- As a workforce analyst, I want the measured local absorption rate compared with the illustrative twenty percent from the source analysis, so that our workforce plan uses our own evidence and states its confidence.
- As a finance analyst, I want the value of absorption stated separately for fee-for-service and value-based contracts, so that we do not book savings that our contracts turn into revenue loss.
